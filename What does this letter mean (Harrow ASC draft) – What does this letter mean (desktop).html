What does this letter mean?

Choose the letter or form you have received. We will explain what it is, where you are in the process, what you need to do now, and who to contact.

What have you received?

Tell us about changes within one month

If your income, savings, benefits or care change, tell us within one month. If you tell us late, a lower charge only starts from the day you tell us, but a higher charge goes back to the date of the change.
